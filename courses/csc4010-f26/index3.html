I am a Professor at the IT University of Copenhagen, working in algorithms and data structures, with particular interests in graph algorithms. I have known Dr.\ Dudeja as a graduate student and have followed her work in theoretical computer science with great interest. In my assessment, she is an exceptionally strong young researcher with an already distinguished record in algorithms. Dr.\ Dudeja works on algorithm design and complexity theory for modern models of computation, including dynamic, streaming, distributed, massively parallel, and sublinear-time models. Her research addresses fundamental questions arising from massive and evolving data sets, especially in graph algorithms. This is a technically challenging and important area, requiring both the development of sophisticated algorithmic techniques and a deep understanding of lower bounds and computational limitations. A central theme of Dr.\ Dudeja's research is matching in evolving graphs. Her PhD dissertation at Rutgers University, entitled \textit{Matching in Evolving Graphs}, and her subsequent work demonstrate sustained expertise and intellectual independence in dynamic graph algorithms. Her research has appeared in premier theoretical computer science venues, including STOC, SODA, ICALP, PODC, CCC, and IPCO. These are among the most selective and influential venues in the field. Dr.\ Dudeja has made several notable contributions to dynamic matching and related problems. In particular, her single-author ICALP 2024 paper, \textit{Decremental Matching in General Weighted Graphs}, illustrates her ability to independently address difficult foundational problems. Her sequence of papers on dynamic matching at STOC 2021, ICALP 2022, SODA 2025, and ICALP 2024 shows both depth and continuity of contribution. More recently, her work on a weighted-to-unweighted reduction for matroid intersection was accepted to IPCO 2026 and invited to a special issue of \textit{Mathematical Programming B}. \end{firstpageform} \newpage % ================================================== % PAGE 2 % ================================================== \begin{secondpageform} Her CCC 2026 paper was likewise invited to a special issue of \textit{ACM Transactions on Computation Theory}. Such invitations provide further evidence of the high quality and broader significance of her research. Her contributions also extend beyond dynamic matching. Her SODA 2025 work on randomized greedy online edge coloring advances understanding of a longstanding question in online algorithms. Her broader research portfolio includes streaming algorithms, distributed stochastic graph algorithms, online matching, and complexity-theoretic problems. This breadth, combined with her clear technical specialization, is a particularly valuable quality in an early-career researcher. Dr.\ Dudeja is also developing into an independent research leader. She has been invited to present her work at universities and research institutions across Europe, North America, India, and China, and has actively participated in highly selective international workshops, including several Dagstuhl seminars. She has begun mentoring junior researchers and has already produced high-quality research with mentees. These accomplishments demonstrate not only her research ability, but also her potential to develop a vigorous and internationally connected research program. Dr.\ Dudeja's appointment as a tenure-track Assistant Professor at the School of Data Science, the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, reflects the strong confidence that leading institutions place in her potential. Her research agenda is highly relevant to the development of algorithmic foundations for modern data science, particularly for large-scale, distributed, and dynamically changing data. In conclusion, Dr.\ Dudeja possesses the originality, technical depth, independence, and international research profile expected of an outstanding young scholar. I am confident that she will make further influential contributions to theoretical computer science, algorithms, and data science.
